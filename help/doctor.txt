Usage: rigorkit doctor

Reports the health of your installations without changing them:
the clone's version and local changes, custom scripts and packs that shadow
official ones, the global record's version with its edited or missing copies
and packs no longer shipped, and for every registered project its version,
whether it is outdated, installed from a development clone, has edited
copies, or no longer exists.

The only repair is the execute bit of official helper scripts in the clone;
custom scripts are only reported.

Options:
  --help                Show this help

Examples:
  rigorkit doctor

Writes:
  Nothing, except the execute bit of official helper scripts.

Exit status: 0 when nothing needs attention, 1 when something does
(outdated, edited, missing, shadowing, local changes, unreadable records),
2 for invalid arguments.
